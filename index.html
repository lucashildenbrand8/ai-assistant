AI Assistant

A private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar to give a morning brief of the day's events.
  • Reads recent Gmail (sender, subject, preview) to point out messages that may need an answer and to suggest labels.
  • Suggests calendar events, email labels and replies. Nothing is created, labelled, archived or sent until the owner approves it on the assistant's private dashboard.
  • Can send the owner optional alerts in their own Telegram chat.

Who can use it

Only its owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and its dashboard is reachable only on the owner's private network.

Privacy policy

AI Assistant is a personal project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
